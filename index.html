🎨🎵 Synesthesia

Sound and image generated together from one point in a ~500-gene space, and steered by you. The picture is a reaction-diffusion simulation (like the one up top) that listens: it breathes with loudness, flashes on every hit and grows new patterns on onsets.

🏺 ClayForm

Pottery and slip-casting molds, from parameters to STL. Pick a classic form, set real dimensions in millimetres, decorate it, and get a file to 3D-print in clay.

  • 🫖Four families from historical references: pot, bowl, cup, vase (amphora, krater, kylix, skyphos…)
  • 〰️Relief waves along the height, around the axis or in a spiral, woven or modulated together
  • 🛞Roller-stamp bands: rope, zigzag, six kinds of Greek meander, or your own picture
  • 🧱Exports the vessel itself or the plaster mold for slip casting
